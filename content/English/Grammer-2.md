名詞片語
	名詞當形容詞用，只能單數
	ex.The soup has vegetables in it.
	It is **vegetable soup**.
複合形容詞
	數字或時間複合形容詞只能+ 單數N.
	ex.The test lasted two hours.
	It was a **two hour** test

one, each, every -> 單數N
two, both, a couple of , a few, several, a number of -> 複數N
a liitle, much, a great deal of -> N(u)

Note:
	only a few(複數)/ a little(N(u))一些
	very few/ little 幾乎沒有
