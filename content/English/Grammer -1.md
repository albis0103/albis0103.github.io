
集合名詞 ex.audience, family
- 單數：是為整體
- 複數：成員們
 ex.
 My family is sweet.
 My family are loving/ well-educated.
 The faculty has chosen a new president.
 The faculty are preparing for classes.

時間、距離單位
- 沒實際經過（概念）：單數
- 有實際經過：複數
ex. Is eight hour of sleep enough for teenagers
