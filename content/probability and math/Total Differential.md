Single Variable Differential:
	$f(x+\Delta x)=f(x)+f'(x)\Delta x$ 
	$f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)=f'(x)\Delta x$  
	$df = f'(x)dx$ 

Double Variable $z = f(x, y)$
$dz = f_xdx+f_ydy$
$f(x+\Delta x, y + \Delta y) = f(x, y)+\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\Delta x+\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\Delta y$ 

Total Defferential
$f:\mathbf{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbf{R}, \mathbf{x}=(x_1, \cdots, x_n)$ , differentiable 

$df = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}dx1+\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}dx2+\cdots +\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_n}dx_n$
	$=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}dx_i$
