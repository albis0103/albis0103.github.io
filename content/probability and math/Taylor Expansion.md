 

$$f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{-\infty}\frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n=f(a)+f'(x-a)+\frac{f''(a)}{2!}(x-a)^2\cdots$$


when $a=0$, Maclaurin's series
- $$e^x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n!}=1+x+\frac{x^2}{2!}\cdots$$$$=lim_{n \rightarrow \infty}(1+\frac{x}{n}^n)$$ 
- $$sinx=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}=x-\frac{x^3}{3!}+\frac{x^5}{5!}\cdots$$ 
- $$cosx=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!}=x-\frac{x^2}{2!}+\frac{x^4}{4!}\cdots$$ 
$$ln(1+x) = x + O(x^2)\approx x$$
