
by SDF definition, for all asset(include risk-free, risk)
$$E_t[M_{t+1}R_{i,t+1}]=1$$
 Risk-free
 $$R_fE[M_{t+1}] = 1, \quad R_f = \frac{1}{E[M_{t+1}]}$$

Risk [[Joint Distribution]]（by $Cov(X,Y) = E(XY)-E(X)E(Y)$）
$$E_t[M_{i}R_{i,t+1}]=E_t[M_{i}]E[R_{i,t+1}]+\text{Cov}(M_{t+1},R_{i,t+1})=1$$
used $E(M_{t+1}) = \frac{1}{R_f}$ 

$$\frac{E[R_{i,t+1}]}{R_f}+\text{Cov}(M_{t+1},R_{i,t+1})=1$$
so:
$$E_t[R_{i,t+1}]-R_f = -R_f\text{Cov}(M_{t+1},R_{i,t+1})$$
