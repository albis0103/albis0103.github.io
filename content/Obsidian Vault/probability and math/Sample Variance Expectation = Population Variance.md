
$$(n-1)S^2=\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X_n -\mu+\mu)^2$$
	$$=\sum_{i=1}^n((X_i-\mu)(\bar X_n -\mu))^2$$
	
$$=\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\mu)^2+\sum_{i=1}^n(\bar X_n-\mu)^2-2\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\mu)(\bar X_n -\mu)$$
	$\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\mu)=n(\bar X_n -\mu)$ 
	
$$=\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\mu)^2+n(\bar X_n-\mu)^2-2n(\bar X_n -\mu)^2$$
so $(n-1)S^2=\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\mu)^2-n(\bar X_n -\mu)^2$ 

$$(n-1)E(S^2)=\sum_{i=1}^nE[(X_i-\mu)^2]-nE[(\bar X_n -\mu)^2]$$
$$=n\sigma^2-nVar(\bar X_n)=(n-1)\sigma^2$$
